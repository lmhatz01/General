## Answer in one paragraph

Your primary goal is motorsport engineering, so the technical profile matters most. That profile is Formula SAE (FSAE) leadership, CAD and manufacturing portfolio, and sound fundamentals. Rank **Monash Mechanical Engineering (Honours) as a single degree, with finance electives, plus Monash Motorsport** first. The Engineering/Commerce (Finance) double degree is second. It is a reasonable option, but it is not automatically better, and the "three units per semester" plan has a hidden cost (see below). Then come Melbourne, Swinburne and RMIT, followed by interstate and overseas options.

## The double-degree question, answered directly

**Verdict: for F1/motorsport as the primary goal, the single degree plus finance electives wins. The double degree is only better if finance is genuinely 40% of what you want.**

- **Technical depth.** The engineering core is the same in both [Assumption]. The cost of the double is time and energy, not missing subjects.
- **WAM.** A heavier concurrent load usually lowers marks. Many F1 and engineering graduate programs filter on grades, but FSAE experience and a portfolio often matter more [Assumption].
- **Duration trap.** If the double is about 5 years at the standard 4 units a semester, then 3 units a semester takes roughly 6.5-7 years [Assumption, arithmetic from "about 5 years"]. That is a very long degree. Check the exact credit points. Also check whether the lower load still counts as full-time for HECS-HELP and any Centrelink payments.
- **FSAE capacity.** A smaller load frees time, but extending the degree to do so is a poor trade. The single degree gives the same freedom in 4 years.
- **Finance later.** Engineering alone does not close the door. Finance graduate programs and postgraduate study (for example a Master of Finance) accept engineers [Assumption]. Check this with Monash Careers.
- **Cost.** An extra year or more costs fees plus a lost year of graduate salary. Check the HECS-HELP rates.

**Decision rule:** if you cannot name the finance job you want, take the single degree. You can take commerce electives and still apply to the double later if Monash allows a transfer, which you must check.

## Where your plan is weak

1. **No Specialist Mathematics.** You are doing Methods, which is normally the prerequisite. First-year engineering maths may be harder without Specialist [Assumption]. Check what bridging support Monash offers.
2. **Realism about F1.** Few F1 roles exist in Australia. Most are in the UK, with visa and right-to-work limits. Realistic pathways include Australian motorsport (Supercars, categories using FSAE-style skills), automotive, aerospace and defence. Plan for those too [Assumption].
3. **The finance fallback is an assumption.** You have not tested whether you like finance.
4. **ATAR.** Your target is not stated. Do not choose by cut-off alone. Check VTAC for current selection ranks and any adjustment schemes (such as school or equity schemes).
