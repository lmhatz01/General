ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] Ranking contradicts itself on RMIT versus the interstate universities
Where: Section 1, item 5: "UQ, UNSW, Sydney and RMIT as interstate or local fallbacks, in that order of 'only if the location suits'". Compare Section 9: RMIT is 5th, ahead of Deakin, and "Put interstate options above this list only if you really want to move."
Problem: Section 1 ranks RMIT below UQ, UNSW and Sydney. Section 9 ranks it above all of them. A Melbourne student cannot tell which order the answer actually recommends. "Location suits" is also meaningless for RMIT, which is in Melbourne. The task asks for a ranked recommendation, and the ranking is not stable. There is no stated reason why Swinburne outranks Melbourne but RMIT sits below it.

ATTACK 2 [MAJOR] Cost and HECS-HELP are not analysed
Where: Section 8: "Whether the course is Commonwealth Supported and how HECS-HELP applies... Check studyassist.gov.au." Also the table row "Cost / HECS-HELP: Lower total debt".
Problem: The task requires coverage of "cost/HECS-HELP" and asks for the duration, opportunity cost and graduate outcomes of the three-units-per-semester plan. The answer gives no structure (Commonwealth-supported places, the HELP loan mechanism, what an extra year costs in order of magnitude or in forgone salary). It only says the cost is "higher". The answer says only "more than five years", so Lukas cannot weigh it.

Where: Section 5 "A practical route": "enrol in the single degree... ask the faculty in writing about adding Commerce later" and Section 9 (single first, double second).
