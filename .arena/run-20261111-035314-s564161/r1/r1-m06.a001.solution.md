## 2. Open questions and how I resolved them

1. **Target ATAR unknown.** Assume "strong" makes him competitive for Monash Engineering. Ranking does not use a number. If his rank lands below the engineering cut-off, ranks 3 to 5 move up.
2. **Location.** Assumed he lives in Melbourne and wants to stay. Interstate options need a clear advantage to justify rent and relocation.
3. **Is F1 literal?** F1 teams are overwhelmingly UK-based. The realistic route is Honours mechanical engineering plus a strong Formula SAE record, then Supercars, F2/F3, OEM or Tier 1, or a later UK step. I treat "technically excellent mechanical engineer with a built-car portfolio" as the target.
4. **No Specialist Mathematics.** The biggest unflagged weakness. Methods is the usual stated prerequisite, but first-year engineering mathematics moves quickly into vectors, complex numbers and multivariable calculus. Plan a refresher (section 8) and consider a bridging unit if the Handbook offers one.
5. **Money or time?** Fees are HECS-HELP, so the real costs are years and focus.
6. **Does finance require a finance degree?** No (section 3).
7. **Product Design and Technologies (PDT).** Used below: it is evidence of CAD, material and manufacturing literacy. His SAT folio and design process are directly usable in a Formula SAE recruitment application and as an early portfolio. Economics gives a real head start for first-year finance and economics electives, which weakens the "I need the degree to learn the content" argument.

## 3. Dialectic: double degree or single degree

**Strongest case for the double (Engineering Honours / Commerce).** It keeps both careers open, gives a finance credential graduate programs screen on, and is a standard Monash combination [Recalled, high; about 5 years full time, check].

**Strongest case for the single.** Motorsport hiring rewards technical depth and a portfolio: a car you built, a kinematics model, a drawing set with tolerances, DAQ analysis. Formula SAE in build season approaches a part-time job and competes for the same hours as commerce units. WAM drives Honours class, scholarships, graduate shortlists and later applications. Finance is the easier thing to add later (postgraduate finance study, analyst roles that hire engineers), while engineering is the harder one.

**Synthesis.** Technical profile wins whenever hours conflict, because motorsport is the primary goal. So the decision is the single degree with a deliberate finance elective stream and economics units. The double is the right answer only if he finds he wants finance as a co-equal career.

**Is Commerce addable later?** Probably, via transfer or a postgraduate route, but the rules are not verified. Check both the Monash double-to-single and single-to-double internal transfer rules and the Master of Finance entry rule. This is the single most valuable unverified fact in the plan, because it governs how reversible the choice is.

**The "three units per semester" idea.** A double is about 40 units [Recalled, high; check]. At 6 units a year it takes about 6.7 years instead of 5: about 1.7 extra years of lost graduate earnings, delayed capstone, and delayed senior Formula SAE roles. It solves workload without improving the degree. Only worth it for health or work reasons, not as a Formula SAE strategy. The extra time also does not remove the industrial experience requirement (Engineers Australia expects about 12 weeks [Recalled, low]).

**HECS-HELP.** Loan covers the student contribution for a Commonwealth Supported Place; indexed, not interest-bearing. Contribution rates are set nationally by discipline band, so the same unit costs the same at any public university. Check rates and repayment rules at studyassist.gov.au. Longer duration means more years of charges and delayed repayment-free earning.

## 5. Ranked recommendation

Ranking criteria, in order: (1) technical depth and motorsport-specific build experience, (2) Formula SAE team strength and access, (3) finance flexibility, (4) cost, time and relocation, (5) entry risk.

**Rank 1. Monash Bachelor of Engineering (Honours), Mechanical specialisation, with a planned finance and economics elective stream, plus Monash Motorsport.**
- Wins on criteria 1, 2 and 4: Melbourne-based, large mechanical cohort, and hosts Monash Motorsport, one of the strongest Australian Formula SAE teams [Recalled, high on existence; results Recalled, low].
- 4 years full time [Recalled, high].
- Finance via electives or a minor, depending on what the Handbook allows. Check that Commerce units are open to engineering students and that prerequisites do not block them.
- Weakness: finance credential thinner than a full degree. Mitigate with economics and finance electives plus later postgraduate options.

**Rank 2. Monash Engineering (Honours) / Commerce double degree.**
- Wins on criterion 3 and loses on 1, 2 and 4. It is the right call only if finance becomes a co-equal goal. Full time about 5 years [Recalled, high].

**Rank 3. RMIT Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) (Mechanical).** Local industry links and a hands-on reputation, and a Formula SAE team, Redback Racing [Recalled, high on name]. Ranked above Swinburne on criterion 2 (a long-standing, visible team) and similar on others. Check CAD, manufacturing and workshop content and any placement option.

**Rank 4. University of Melbourne, Bachelor of Science (engineering systems pathway) then Master of Engineering (Mechanical).** [Recalled, low on pathway name; confirm in the Melbourne Handbook.] A 3+2 structure gives a bachelor's at year 3, which is a finance hedge, and breadth subjects. It has a student team (Melbourne Uni Racing [Recalled, low]). It ranks below RMIT here because it is 5 years to the accredited qualification, front-loads science before engineering design, and conditions Master's entry on bachelor marks. It would rise if his priority were research, modelling, or the brand, or if he is above the Melbourne cut-off and below Monash.

**Rank 5. Swinburne Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) (Mechanical).** Known for an industry-based learning placement option [Recalled, low]. Placed below RMIT because the student team's current activity level is unconfirmed (Swinburne has had a Formula SAE entry [Recalled, low]). Rises if a paid placement year is more valuable to him than team-based build experience.

**Rank 6. Deakin Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) (Mechanical).** Geelong and Waurn Ponds campus, a student team (Deakin Motorsport [Recalled, low]). Tuition is NOT cheaper (national bands), so its only advantage is location for a student living near Geelong. Otherwise it offers nothing clearly better than ranks 1 to 5.

**Rank 7. UNSW Sydney Mechanical Engineering (Honours).** UNSW Racing is reputed to be one of the strongest Formula SAE teams in the country [Recalled, low on current standing]. It is ranked last only because it requires relocation (rent, a separate UAC application, being away from family) and Monash gives a comparable team without moving. Moves up if he is willing to relocate and values team strength above all.

**Not ranked (interstate, no clear advantage over Monash to justify relocating).** University of Sydney (Sydney University Formula SAE team [Recalled, low]), University of Queensland (UQ Racing [Recalled, high on name]), and the University of Adelaide, now part of Adelaide University from 2026 [Recalled, low] (its team, Adelaide University Motorsport [Recalled, low]). They are academically strong and have credible teams. I exclude them on cost and relocation, not on a claim that their teams are weak. Revisit only if he wants to leave Victoria.

**Overseas undergraduate.** Not justified: international fees and visas. A realistic overseas step is a postgraduate year after strong Honours results and a Formula SAE record (for example UK motorsport-focused MSc programs such as Cranfield or Oxford Brookes [Recalled, low]).

## 7. What would change the ranking

- Selection rank below the Monash engineering cut-off: ranks 3 to 5 become the working list, with Melbourne rising if he is above its cut-off.
- He decides finance is co-equal: double moves to Rank 1.
- Monash Motorsport recruitment is closed to first years or needs more hours than he can give: re-weigh RMIT.
- Monash transfer rules say Commerce cannot be added later: the double gains real option value.
- Official pages show changed duration, structure or prerequisites: rerun the comparison.
- A scholarship or guaranteed placement elsewhere.
