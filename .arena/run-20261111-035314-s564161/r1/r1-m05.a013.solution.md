## 3. The double-degree question, answered

**For your stated primary goal, do the single Engineering (Honours) degree with finance-flavoured electives. Do not default to Engineering + Commerce.**

| Criterion | Eng only | Eng + Commerce (Finance) | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technical depth | Whole elective space for dynamics, controls, CAD/FEA, materials, manufacturing | About one extra year of units sits in commerce; same core engineering, fewer technical electives/projects in a given time | Eng |
| WAM | One faculty's workload; easier to protect | Two assessment cultures; check whether honours/postgraduate selection uses engineering-only or combined WAM [Check] | Eng |
| FSAE capacity | Can hold a lead role for 2-3 seasons | At full load, poor; at three units a semester, possible | Eng |
| Duration | 4 years full time [Check] | About 5 years full time; about 6-7 at three units a semester [Check] | Eng |
| Cost | Fewer units | Extra units are in a higher contribution band (below); delayed salary | Eng |
| Finance optionality | Good (below) | Best | Double (narrowly) |
| Motorsport hiring | Same or better (more project time) | No visible advantage | Eng |
| Graduate finance/consulting signal | Fine | Stronger signal, not required | Double |

**Cost, stated correctly.** Fees are charged per unit, so spreading the same units over more semesters does not itself add tuition. The extra cost of the double is (a) the additional Commerce units, about one extra year's worth, (b) a year or more of delayed engineering salary, and (c) the longer HELP debt balance. Engineering and commerce/business sit in different Commonwealth student-contribution bands, with business/commerce historically the higher band, so the added units cost more per unit than engineering units. I will not quote dollar figures. [Check the current band amounts on the Australian Government StudyAssist site and each university's fee page.] HECS-HELP is a loan: the debt is indexed each 1 June, and repayment is income-contingent once you earn above the threshold, so a longer degree mostly shifts when repayments start and how long the balance is indexed. Recent changes to indexation and to how repayment thresholds work were legislated or proposed in 2025; confirm the current rules. [Check]

**Where your plan is weak or assumption-based:**
- "A double gives flexibility" is option value bought in advance, mostly cheaply available later (graduate diploma, master's, CFA, internal transfer).
- Stretching to three units a semester fixes weekly hours but turns a 4-year problem into a 6-7-year one, and you pay for a credential you call secondary.
- Engineering does give a credible route into finance: quantitative risk, energy trading, engineering consulting, corporate finance at engineering-heavy firms value Methods-level maths, modelling and optimisation. [General knowledge, not verified for specific employers.] Economics, which you already study, helps with the interview language.
- Better middle path: engineering plus finance/economics electives or a minor, if the structure allows. [Check whether Monash lets you take breadth electives or a minor and how many units.]

**When the double would be right:** you find, after first-year engineering, that finance is your preferred career, or you want a motorsport commercial/strategy path. Ask Monash whether you can move from the single degree to the double (or back) after first year without losing credit. [Check. This is the most valuable email you can send.]

## 7. What would change the ranking

- **Very high ATAR and a wish to keep every door open:** Melbourne rises (to 2-3), and the Monash double becomes more defensible.
- **Weak first-semester enjoyment of engineering maths/physics:** the cheapest time to test the double or a finance-leaning route.
- **Monash Motorsport recruitment goes badly, or the team's actual build opportunities are thin:** Swinburne and RMIT overtake on the team criterion. Change the team score by 2 and rank 2 can reach 1.
- **Verified team results or placements:** if UNSW's record and Sydney industry access are verified as clearly stronger, and you can fund living costs, UNSW can tie rank 1.
- **Finance becomes the primary goal:** commerce-first options (Melbourne, Monash) outrank everything here.
- **A scholarship or paid cadetship** at any university can outweigh a 30-40-point gap.

## 8. Subject-level guidance

I cannot verify Monash unit codes. I list the sequence by topic, with recalled codes marked **low confidence [Check in the Handbook]**. For each topic the contribution to motorsport is specific.

| Stage | Topic (recalled code if any) | Contribution to a motorsport profile |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | Engineering maths (ENG1005 recalled), foundation maths if you lack Specialist (ENG1090 recalled) | Calculus, linear algebra and ODEs underpin every vehicle-dynamics and control model; without them second-year dynamics becomes a bottleneck |
| Year 1 | Mechanics (ENG1014 recalled), engineering design/modelling units (ENG101x family recalled) | Statics and dynamics foundations; first CAD and team design experience, the basis of an FSAE portfolio |
| Year 2 | Dynamics, vibrations (MEC24xx recalled) | Suspension kinematics and compliance, ride and damper tuning, lap-time simulation inputs |
| Year 2 | Solid mechanics, materials | Wishbone/chassis sizing, FEA setup, understanding failure modes |
| Year 2-3 | Thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, heat transfer | Powertrain thermal limits, cooling, and the physics behind aerodynamics |
| Year 2-3 | Machine design, design methodology, GD&T/tolerancing | Parametric CAD, assemblies, drawings, DFM/DFA, the cleanest match to your stated CAD goals |
| Year 3 | Control systems, signal processing | Traction/engine control, filtering and calibrating data-acquisition channels |
| Year 3-4 | Numerical methods, optimisation, computational modelling | Lap-time optimisation, solver literacy for FEA/CFD, model fitting to test data; also directly useful in quant finance |
| Year 3-4 | Manufacturing/machining, composites (elective) | Fabrication judgement that separates you from theory-only graduates |
| Year 4 | Final-year/capstone team project | Documented engineering output with a result |

**Test for MMA3101** (I cannot confirm what it is). Open its Handbook entry and answer:
1. Is the content mathematical modelling/numerical methods, mechanics/dynamics, materials, manufacturing, control, or CAD? Yes to any: score it.
2. Does it include hands-on or project assessment that produces something you can show? If yes, higher value.
3. Does it have prerequisites you would meet by the year you want it?
4. Does it replace a unit that you would otherwise take on that list? If it displaces a core dynamics/control/design unit, skip it.
5. If its content is general business, management or unrelated breadth, it does not help the motorsport profile; if you need elective credit, prefer the topics above.

Rule: take it only if it scores yes on question 1 and does not fail question 4.

**Skill source, so you do not confuse them:** direct engineering skill development (the units above; FSAE design/build/test roles); transferable practical intuition (machining, wiring, track days, kart or club motorsport; helpful, not a credential); mere interest (watching F1; no employer weight).
