ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] Skills and subjects section does not give the subject-level or "exactly how" advice the task requires
Where: "Skills map: what builds what" table ("Engineering design units", "Mathematics and computational modelling units in engineering", "Dynamics and mechanics units") and "For each subject you consider, ask whether it adds one of these skills."
Problem: The task says "wants subject-code-level advice where possible" and "Explain exactly how each contributes to a motorsport pathway rather than just calling it 'useful'." The table only names a generic unit category for each skill and tags it "Direct". It never explains how maths, numerical methods, control, optimisation or materials feed a motorsport task (for example, how ODEs and numerical methods become lap-time simulation, or how dynamics becomes load transfer). It names no Monash mechanical-specialisation topics at all. It also pushes the work back to Lukas ("Look up the actual code and unit guide"). He cannot act on "Engineering design units" without guessing which ones. The task's favoured list (computational modelling, optimisation, numerical methods, data analysis) is mostly absent as separate items.

ATTACK 2 [MAJOR] Cost and duration reasoning for the spread-out double degree is wrong or muddled
Where: "Duration trap" and "Cost" bullets: "An extra year or more costs fees plus a lost year of graduate salary"; "check whether the lower load still counts as full-time for HECS-HELP and any Centrelink payments."
Problem: In a Commonwealth-supported double degree, tuition is charged per unit. Stretching the same units over more semesters at 3 units per semester does not add a year of fees. The real cost of spreading is delayed salary and longer debt exposure. The extra units of the commerce half are the only extra tuition. The text treats "extra year" and "extra fees" as the same thing. It also ties HECS-HELP eligibility to full-time load. HECS-HELP applies per enrolled unit, whatever the load, so that check sends Lukas after the wrong question. The task lists "using HECS-HELP" and "cost/HECS-HELP" as items to cover. The answer gives only "Check the HECS-HELP rates" and no explanation of what HECS-HELP does or does not cover.

ATTACK 3 [MAJOR] "Single degree gives the same freedom in 4 years" does not answer the three-units-per-semester question
Where: "FSAE capacity. A smaller load frees time, but extending the degree to do so is a poor trade. The single degree gives the same freedom in 4 years."
Problem: This claim is unsupported and probably false. A single degree at a normal full load of 4 units per semester leaves less free time per semester than a double degree at 3 units per semester. The two options differ in weekly study hours, not only in years. The answer never compares weekly load (about 12 units versus 9 or 12 contact and study hours) or the FSAE time commitment. The task asks the double degree to be analysed on "extracurricular capacity" and "duration". The claim is stated as fact with no confidence tag, even though the answer tags everything else [Assumption] or [Recall].
