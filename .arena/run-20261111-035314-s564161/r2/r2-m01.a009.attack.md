ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] Spreading the double is never quantified, and cost is conflated with duration
Where: Section 3 table rows "Duration" and "Cost"; Section 6 "Cost": "a longer double means more units, probably at a higher band for the Commerce half"
Problem: The task says the student is weighing "spreading the double degree over a longer period (about three units per semester)" and using HECS-HELP. The solution gives only "Roughly 5 to 6 years ... longer at reduced load" and never works out the 3-units-per-semester timeline (about 4 x 4/3, so roughly 6.7 years for a 5-year double). It also says a longer double "means more units". That is wrong. Spreading the same units over more semesters does not add units, so it does not add tuition. Only the extra Commerce units do. The cost comparison the student asked for is therefore muddled. The real cost of spreading is delayed graduate income and indexation on an unpaid debt, and the solution never states this.
