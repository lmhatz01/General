ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] The ranking is justified by team strength, but the team evidence was never gathered
Where: Section 6, "Ranking is by fit to the primary goal (motorsport/mechanical engineering with strong technical depth and team access)"; Section 1 says the only confirmed fact is Monash Motorsport's size.
Problem: The stated ranking criterion is team access, yet the solution admits it never checked any other team. Melbourne is dropped to 5 because "I cannot verify that its Formula SAE team matches Monash's". UQ is placed at 6 on "widely regarded ... [recall; verify]". Deakin, Swinburne, Sydney and RMIT team names are tagged [low-confidence] or [unknown]. It says a web search worked for Monash Motorsport, but it ran no equivalent search for MUR, UQ Racing, Redback, RMIT Racing, Swinburne or Deakin. The ranking of nine options therefore rests on recall plus an unexamined criterion, and the student is told to go and check the evidence himself. The task asks for "their Formula SAE / motorsport teams, industry links" to be considered, and this does not do it.

Where: Section 4, "I give no ATARs or selection ranks"; ATAR context "Melbourne's engineering pathway generally has a higher selection rank than Monash, which is generally higher than RMIT, Swinburne and Deakin".
Problem: The task says "Cover entry requirements (ATAR/selection rank, prerequisites ..., adjustment factors/schemes)" and "state which facts you verified".
