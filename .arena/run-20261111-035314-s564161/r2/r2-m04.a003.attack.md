ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] The central "single first, add Commerce later" advice never tests the step-down option
Where: Section 3 ("'A double gives flexibility' is option value bought in advance, mostly cheaply available later") and Section 6 ("single above double, and use the post-first-year transfer route if you later want commerce").
Problem: The whole double-versus-single answer rests on single-to-double transfer being easy (a graduate diploma, master's or CFA are weaker substitutes). That is only flagged "[Check]" and is never analysed against the opposite route. Section 6 itself says the double probably needs a rank at least as high as the single. It then never considers the obvious hedge that follows: list the double first, then drop Commerce after year 1 if WAM or Formula SAE suffers. Entering the double at VTAC and stepping down is a different option from stepping up later, and it may be the cheaper one. The user explicitly asked whether the double is "automatically superior", and a hedge-purchase analysis that ignores one direction of the hedge is half done. Counterexample: if Monash allows step-down but not step-up, the recommended VTAC order forecloses the double for good, and the answer says nothing about that risk.

ATTACK 2 [MAJOR] The scoring matrix contradicts the prose and does not score the option the student asked about
Where: Section 4 table, row 7 (Monash double: Depth 4, Team 4, Workload 2) against the text "Its team access is the same as the single's" and the Section 3 table ("At three units a semester, possible").
Problem: The prose says team access is identical, yet the double is scored 4 on Team against 5 for the single. That 25-point gap is a third of the 100-point difference between rank 1 and rank 7. Depth is also marked down (4 versus 5) although Section 3 says "same core engineering". The only variant scored is full load (Workload 2). The variant the student is actually weighing, about three units a semester, is never scored. Scored as a 6-7 year degree with Workload 4, the double's total would rise to about 360 and move it up past UNSW (365 is the nearest). The 7th place that the bottom line relies on ("it does not [add motorsport value]") is therefore produced by unexplained score choices. The answer says gaps under 40 points are "within my scoring noise", but it still states a strict ordinal ranking and builds the VTAC list from it.

ATTACK 3 [MAJOR] Cost and HECS-HELP requirement is met with no numbers, and one claim is misleading
Where: Section 3 "Cost, stated correctly": "I will not quote dollar figures" and "spreading the same units over more semesters does not itself add tuition".
Problem: The task requires "cost/HECS-HELP". The student gets no band amounts, no total for 4 years versus 5 years versus 6.7 years, and no repayment threshold. The statement that spreading does not add tuition is incomplete. Student contribution amounts are indexed annually, so the same units taken later are charged at later, higher nominal rates. Refusing figures is defensible, but the answer gives not even order-of-magnitude comparisons (for example engineering versus commerce band per year), so the student cannot compare options. "Recent changes ... were legislated or proposed in 2025" is not usable: it does not say which of the two applies or what changed.
