# Defense: the single degree (E3001) should go ahead

1. **The retrial facts remove the double's only real argument.** The double was a hedge. Now "Finance is only a backup career" and switching is "NOT possible". The review itself says double only "if finance a genuine co-career" (Option C). Lukas has said it is not, so five years of locked-in cost buys insurance he does not intend to claim.

2. **Finance can be added later and cheaply; motorsport timing cannot.** The record says finance is "Fixable later via master's or CFA after 2-3 years of work", and "Most of the finance signal [is] obtainable with 3-4 well-chosen units, a financial modelling project and a finance society role." Objection: banking is "Hard without Commerce signalling", but that is the backup path, and PE/VC is "mostly post-experience" anyway.

3. **The single degree fits what F1 and FSAE actually select on.** McLaren wants "an engineering, maths or science degree with minimum 2:1"; Mercedes lists "Formula Student". "Neither mentions commerce." Grade and FSAE ownership drive hiring. The single gets Design 1 in Year 2, not Year 3, and finishes FEA and the project in Year 4, not Year 5. If the older rule that technical students join "from second year" holds, the single arrives with a design unit and the double does not. That rule is unconfirmed.

4. **The single protects WAM and FSAE time.** Lukas has no Specialist Maths, and "Maths is the WAM risk in first year." The double is "heaviest load, delayed design and thermo, no electives"; spread out, it is "50+ hours a week for seven years". The single keeps "about 8 flexible slots" for the commerce units, and may fit CFD (MMA3101, unverified).

5. **The cost gap is large.** Commerce is "Band 4 (higher than engineering...)", and the delayed salary is "well over $150k gross (rough, unsourced)".

**Conceded:** F1 is "low probability from any pathway", so the single does not make F1 likely. It only avoids paying more for a path that does not improve those odds. MMA3101 availability, CSP effects and Monash Motorsport joining rules are unconfirmed.
