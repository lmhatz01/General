## Charges that stuck
1. The "finance can be added later" fallback is unverified, and 11 jurors named it as their swing point.
2. First-year maths without Specialist Maths risks your WAM (grade average), and commerce units add load then (3 jurors).
3. Key facts are unconfirmed: Monash Motorsport joining rules and whether the maps will change (3 jurors).

## What the defense got right
1. You said finance is only a backup, and the review recommends the double only if finance is a genuine co-career. A fifth year buys insurance you don't plan to use.
2. McLaren and Mercedes ask for engineering grades and Formula Student, not commerce. The single also gets Design 1 a year earlier and keeps about 8 flexible slots.

## Conditions for acquittal
1. This week, get sourced evidence on whether engineers move into finance via a master's or CFA. Ask two or three people who did it.
2. Get written Monash answers: can the single's free slots hold 3-4 finance units, is MMA3101 available, and does Monash Motorsport require anything only the double provides.
3. Confirm the Foundation Mathematics (ENG1090) requirement and map a plan keeping finance units out of heavy maths semesters.

## The sentence
The single degree stands. Take E3001, but treat the finance fallback as a claim to check, not a fact. Do the three checks before finalising preferences, and spend free slots on controls and electronics first.
