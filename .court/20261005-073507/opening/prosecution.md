# Prosecution opening

**1. The finance hedge is unsourced, and it is the half of the plan that has to carry the "second pathway."**
The file says the finance career map is "judgement, NOT verified against sources," and that investment banking is "Hard without Commerce signalling." The claim that "most of the finance signal [is] obtainable with 3-4 well-chosen units" is also unsourced. If finance is a real co-career, Lukas drops the hedge on a guess. Cost: a failed second pathway, repaired only by a master's or CFA after 2-3 years of work.

**2. The free slots for A+ do not exist in the numbers given.**
The single has "about 8 flexible slots," but only "first-year breadth, Year 1 elective, 2 in Year 2" fall in Years 1-2. That is 4 slots. Foundation mathematics (ENG1090) and possibly PHS1001 come on top of the map. Putting 3-4 commerce units in those slots uses nearly all of them. Cost: the electives that justify the single degree are spent on the hedge, or the plan needs summer units, which means more load.

**3. The "managed load" erases the single degree's time advantage.**
At 3 units a semester the single is "32 units, ~5.5 years." The double at standard pace is "5 years full-time." Add "3 units = 36 hours study" and FSAE at "15-20+ hours a week," and the load is 51-56 hours a week. The single is only faster if Lukas carries a heavier load. That load is the WAM risk the file flags for a student without Specialist Maths who needs a "minimum 2:1 or equivalent" for McLaren. Cost: either the extra year arrives anyway or the grades suffer.

**4. The case for the single rests on benefits the file itself discounts.**
"Technical electives matter less than the double's delayed core." CFD "isn't the gate to F1 aero." Monash Motorsport's skills are "controls, electronics, data and simulation, not NA engine design." F1 is "low probability from any pathway." The stated goal does not separate the two options. What remains is the "roughly estimated well over $150k" salary figure, which the file calls "rough, unsourced." Cost: the decision turns on one invented-looking number.

**5. The checks that could reverse the choice are all still open.**
Transfer between E3001 and E3005, FSAE joining rules, CSP status at 3 units and MMA3101 availability are "unconfirmed." The course maps are "pending finalisation – subject to change." Cost: Lukas commits before the facts that decide the question are known.
