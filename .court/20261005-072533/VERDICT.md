## Charges that stuck
- No evidence the full Commerce degree beats Mech Eng plus a finance minor, which the defense itself admitted: 11 jurors.
- The plan rests on unchecked Monash rules (CFD access, additional units, maximum duration, commencement-year changes), yet is stated as "I chose": 9 jurors.
- Option C adds two years of delayed income, HECS accrual and WAM risk without adding elective slots: 6 jurors.

## What the defense got right
- The plan fits his real goals: he does not want to optimise purely for F1, and he concedes engineering alone does not make him competitive for elite finance.
- Nothing in the record shows CFD or a six-year duration is blocked, so this is a checking problem, not a proven flaw.

## Conditions for acquittal
1. Get written Monash answers for his commencement year confirming MMA3101 (or similar CFD depth) is available in the double degree without an unfunded additional unit.
2. Get written confirmation that a six-year, three-units-per-semester path is permitted and stays CSP/HECS-HELP funded.
3. Find real evidence from graduate outcomes or Monash careers staff that the full Commerce degree opens finance and consulting roles a minor does not.

## The sentence
Lukas, this plan is not rejected, but it is not ready. This week, email Monash with those questions and compare the full degree against Mech Eng with a finance minor. If the answers are good, proceed with Option C. If not, take the cheaper path.
