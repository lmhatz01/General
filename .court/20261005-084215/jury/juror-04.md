VOTE: NOT GUILTY
REASON: The prosecution proved that the evidence is thin, but it did not prove the plan fails, since engineering-first needs only the modest claim that the degree keeps options open, and the record's own figures (14% in IT outside engineering, finance listed among destinations, 28% UK finance entry equal to other graduates) show no disadvantage.
DECIDING FACT: The record says 28% of UK engineering graduates enter finance and business, the same rate as all other graduates, which shows no finance penalty from engineering alone, and that is all Lukas claimed.
WOULD FLIP IF: The Monash check showed that Commerce cannot be added or dropped later and that the double degree is the only route to finance, while the plan still commits him to engineering only.
