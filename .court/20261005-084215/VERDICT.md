## Charges that stuck
1. Nobody has checked whether Monash lets Commerce be added or dropped later (all 12 jurors leaned on it).
2. A double degree's load might hurt WAM (grade average) and Formula SAE time, unchecked (5 jurors).
3. The evidence is thin: unchecked snippets and a UK finance figure, with no Australian data (5 jurors).

## What the defense got right
1. Engineering-first is low-regret: about 95% of engineering graduates are in full-time work three years out, a cushion if motorsport pay disappoints.
2. The one finance figure (28% in the UK, same as other graduates) shows no penalty for engineering alone, and "keeps doors open" is all Lukas claimed.

## Conditions for acquittal
1. Email Monash this week and get in writing whether Commerce can be added or dropped after starting engineering.
2. Ask Monash and Formula SAE students how the double-degree load affects WAM and build time, and whether finance electives or a minor would do.
3. Say plainly whether he would accept low-paid junior motorsport jobs overseas, and check his visa and work rights there.

## The sentence
The plan may proceed, but not on trust. Lukas should get the Monash answers before choosing a degree structure. If Commerce cannot be added later, he must decide now whether finance matters enough for the double degree. If it can, start with engineering and add Commerce only if it proves worth it.
