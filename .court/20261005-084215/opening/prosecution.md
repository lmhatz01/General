# Prosecution opening

**1. The new evidence does not support the new argument.**
Lukas argues engineering alone "keeps finance and other careers open." The file's own evidence says otherwise: "28% of UK engineering graduates enter finance and business, the same rate as all other graduates." That means no advantage. The Deakin figure says "outside engineering" is "mostly not finance," and "the finance share was not given." Cost: he may drop Commerce on a premise the record does not show. Finance is a secondary pathway, so he could be left without it.

**2. The evidence is too weak to decide anything.**
It comes from snippets, "not independently checked," with "year not confirmed." The record has "no Australian figure for the share of engineering-only graduates who end up in finance" and "no data comparing engineering-only with engineering+Commerce." The anecdotes are UK, Canada and US, with "no percentage." Cost: a choice worth years and HECS-HELP debt rests on survivorship stories.

**3. The decisive facts are unchecked.**
"Whether Commerce can be added or dropped later at Monash, and the effect of the load on WAM and Formula SAE" are "Not yet checked." If Commerce can be added later, the double degree is an unnecessary early cost. If it cannot, the option is lost by default. Either way, he is planning without the rule that sets the cost. Cost: extra years of debt, or a closed door.

**4. The motorsport path has an unaddressed pay and entry problem.**
The record gives UK motorsport graduate starting salaries of "25,000-30,000 pounds" and Newey saying F1 is "no longer the best-paid industry." The new fact says he is "willing to move overseas," but "Whether he would accept low-paid junior roles overseas was not stated." Australian entry median is about $75,000. The case never says how he gets into F1 from Victoria, or whether he would accept that pay gap. Cost: the plan's main route is not tested against its biggest barrier.

**5. The question is framed as a false binary and ignores the thing he controls first.**
Year 12 results gate every option, and the record gives no target ATAR or entry requirement for Monash Engineering or the double degree. "Aiming for strong results" is not a number. Cost: he is optimising a degree structure before knowing which structures he qualifies for.
