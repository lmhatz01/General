# Defense opening

The charge is that the plan should not go ahead as described. It should. Lukas's plan is sound for a Year 12 student, and the choice he calls "motorsport or finance" does not have to be made yet.

1. **The pathway keeps both options open.** The file says: "Monash University, Engineering (Mechanical), either alone or as a double degree with Commerce (Finance)... Also considering engineering with finance electives/minor." Every route starts with the same mechanical engineering degree. The objection is that he hasn't picked a career. But the engineering core is the same for both careers, so the choice can wait until he has real evidence from university.

2. **His interest is concrete and consistent.** "Primary interest: motorsport / F1 engineering, particularly mechanical engineering. Finance/business is a secondary pathway." He names specific skills: "CAD, mechanical design, vehicle dynamics, manufacturing, machining, composites, suspension, aerodynamics, powertrain, data acquisition." The objection is that teenage interests change. But a list this detailed, and the specific Monash electives he has already looked at (MMA3101), suggest researched interest, not a passing fancy.

3. **His subjects fit the plan.** He is taking "Mathematical Methods, Physics, Economics, Product Design and Technologies, English." Maths and Physics are what mechanical engineering needs. Economics and Product Design cover both the finance side and the hands-on side. The objection is that the Economics subject pulls him toward finance. It is better read as cheap insurance than as a sign of conflict.

4. **He has already named the main risk and the ways to manage it.** He worries the double degree "may reduce WAM, time for Monash Formula SAE, and depth of technical engineering skill." His options are to spread it over "about three units per semester", use HECS-HELP, or take only a minor. Formula SAE is the practical experience motorsport employers look for. Knowing a trade-off exists and having several ways to manage it is a strength.

5. **Concession.** The file gives no salary expectations, location preferences or willingness to move overseas. Those matter, especially because motorsport jobs are scarce. They can be added later and do not stop him starting the degree.
