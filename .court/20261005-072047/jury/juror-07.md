VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: I would not hand over $10000 for anything when nobody can tell me where the cash comes from or how the running costs get paid in a slow month, and a student with no stated income has no slow-month cushion at all.
DECIDING FACT: The record gives no income, no savings and no word on whether the $10000 is savings or borrowed, and the defense itself conceded it cannot defend the purchase if the money is borrowed or drains savings.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated that the $10000 is the student's own spare savings, with registration, insurance and a year of running costs separately covered by steady income.
