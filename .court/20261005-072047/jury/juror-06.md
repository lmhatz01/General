VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: As an engineer I would not sign off a build with no load figures, and what breaks first here is running costs (registration, young-driver insurance, repairs) landing on a school student with no stated income, which the defense itself concedes could push the true cost well above $10000.

WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated that the $10000 is the student's own spare savings, with a further budget already set aside for a year of registration, insurance and repairs.
