VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: A stranger reading "Should I buy a $10000 used car?" from a Year 12 student can't tell in five seconds whether it's smart or reckless, because the case leaves out the one thing that makes it matter, which is where the money comes from.
DECIDING FACT: The record gives no income, no savings, and no statement of whether the $10000 is savings or borrowed, and the defense itself concedes it cannot defend the purchase if the money is borrowed or drains all their savings.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated up front that the $10000 is the student's own spare savings, with running costs (registration, insurance, servicing) covered separately.
