VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: The proposal to spend $10000 rests entirely on assumptions that are not in the record, and the defense can only win by supposing facts (own savings, a sound car, covered running costs) that nobody has proved.
DECIDING FACT: The defense conceded it cannot defend the purchase if the money is borrowed or drains the student's savings, and the record is silent on the source of the money, on any income, and on the running costs.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated that the $10000 was the student's own spare savings, with income or funds set aside to cover registration, insurance and repairs, and that they have a licence and a real need for a car.
