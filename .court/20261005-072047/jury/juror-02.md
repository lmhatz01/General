VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: As someone who would actually be handing over $10000 this week, I would not do it when nobody can tell me whether it is my own savings or borrowed money, or whether I even need the car.
DECIDING FACT: The record gives no income, no savings and no source for the $10000, and the defense itself conceded it cannot defend the purchase if the money is borrowed or drains all the student's savings.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated that the $10000 was the student's own spare savings, with running costs (registration, insurance, servicing) separately covered and a real need for the car, such as no other way to get to work or study.
