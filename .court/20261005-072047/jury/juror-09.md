VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: As a parent I would not let my school-age child put $10000 plus insurance, registration and repairs into a car when nobody has said whose money it is, whether they have a licence, or whether they need one.
DECIDING FACT: The record gives no income, savings, or source of the $10000 (savings or borrowed), and the defense itself conceded it cannot defend the purchase if the money is borrowed or drains their savings.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated that the $10000 is the student's own spare savings, with a further budget set aside for a year of insurance, registration and servicing, and that they already hold a licence and have a real need for a car.
