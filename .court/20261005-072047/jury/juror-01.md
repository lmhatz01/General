VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: I follow the money, and the record never says whose $10000 it is, what income covers the registration, insurance and repairs on top, or what the student would give up, so I can't approve spending it as described.

WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated that the $10000 is the student's own spare savings, with a further budget already set aside for a year of registration, insurance and servicing.
