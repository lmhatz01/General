# The verdict: HUNG JURY, 6 to 6

**The case:** Claim on trial: "An engineering degree from a top school like Monash University is versatile for many industries, from motorsports to even finance and consulting."

**The charge:** The case fails as stated: it should not go ahead the way it is described.

## How the jury voted

| Juror | Vote | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The Accountant | GUILTY | A multi-year, multi-thousand-dollar degree choice is being justified by a claim with no figures behind it (no graduate outcomes, no fees, no extra years or lost earnings for a Commerce double degree), and I will not back a spend I cannot cost or source. |

## Charges that stuck
1. No evidence that engineers actually move into finance or consulting, the exact question Lukas asked (6 jurors).
2. "Top school" and "Monash beats others" are asserted, never shown (4 jurors).
3. The claim never says which of his four options to choose (3 jurors).

## What the defense got right
1. The claim is modest: "versatile", not "guaranteed entry". The record is silent on finance, not against it.
2. Two options (Engineering alone, Engineering with finance electives) protect Formula SAE time and depth, and leave Commerce possible later, so the plan does not depend on the finance claim.

## Conditions for acquittal
1. Find Monash graduate-outcome or employer-hiring data (course pages, alumni profiles, Formula SAE alumni) showing mechanical engineers entering finance, consulting and motorsport without Commerce.
2. Compare costs: extra years, fees and WAM risk of the double degree versus finance electives. Check whether Commerce affects Engineers Australia accreditation.
3. Restate the claim as a recommendation ("engineering with finance electives keeps finance open and protects Formula SAE time"), then ask recruiters or senior Formula SAE members whether employers screen out engineering-only graduates.

## The sentence
The claim is unproven, not wrong. Lukas, do not let "versatile" decide for you. This week, gather the outcome data and ask two or three people in finance or motorsport what they look for. Until then, lean engineering-first: it protects what you most want and is cheapest to change later.
