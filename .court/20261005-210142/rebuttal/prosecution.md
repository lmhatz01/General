1. **Fails as proof.** Matching his primary goal shows only that Mechanical Engineering is the obvious route to motorsport, which no one disputes. It says nothing about the contested part, versatility across industries. "Top school" stays unshown, as the defense concedes.

2. **Fails.** The defense concedes there is no evidence that engineers reach finance or consulting. "Keeps options open" is then an assumption, not a finding. Lukas asked exactly this question, and the claim answers it by asserting yes.

3. **Partly survives, and it undercuts the claim.** Adding commerce later only matters if engineering alone is not enough for finance. The record does not say whether electives or a later add-on are as credible as a Commerce degree, or what they cost.

4. **Fails.** The record does not show Commerce would help a finance move more, and equally does not show it would not. Silence cannot favour the single-degree path. The defense also swaps the claim for a different one, that the single degree protects depth and time. The file has no data on WAM, workload or Formula SAE hours.

**The point for the jury: point 2.** Whether engineering alone gives a real route into finance and consulting is the contested part of the claim and Lukas's own stated concern. The defense concedes there is no evidence for it. If the jury finds it unproven, "as stated" fails, whatever is true for motorsport.
